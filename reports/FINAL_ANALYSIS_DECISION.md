# Final analysis decision

## Decision: CONDITIONAL GO

The project can proceed to a manuscript draft focused on negative genome-wide genetic architecture and one Phase 4-upgraded candidate antagonistic locus, L006. The manuscript should include L007 as a concordant internal contrast and should retain the negative HDL-L multiple-testing result as an important boundary condition.

This is not approval for causal, clinical, therapeutic, or independent-replication claims. It is not approval to expand into single-cell, eQTL/TWAS, MR, drug-target, or pathway analyses before an independent validation plan is available.

## Reasons

- The global negative signal is present in the frozen primary and rescue analyses.
- L006 passed quantitative EUR LD QC and unified SuSiE/coloc.susie.
- L006 has PP4 = 0.975590, signal-level CS overlap = 10, and opposite harmonized effects at rs1547014.
- LAVA and HDL-L point in the same local negative direction at L006, but neither local P value is definitive after the broader multiplicity context.
- L007 passed the one-time rescue rule and is concordant, providing a useful negative control for the antagonistic-direction claim.

## Required manuscript safeguards

1. Separate genome-wide negative correlation from locus-level antagonistic evidence.
2. Call L006 a candidate/probable antagonistic locus, not confirmed antagonistic pleiotropy.
3. Describe coloc output as shared-signal support, not proof of a shared causal variant.
4. State that LAVA is an orthogonal sensitivity analysis using the same summary statistics and no explicit sample-overlap matrix.
5. State that POAG IOP/non-IOP components are dependent decompositions and are not independent replication cohorts.
6. Keep clinical and therapeutic implications as future validation questions only.
