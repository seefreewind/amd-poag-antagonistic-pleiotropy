# Paper story lock after Phase 4

## Main claim

Advanced AMD and POAG show a negative genome-wide genetic correlation. A focused quantitative-LD adjudication of the strongest opposite-direction locus, L006, supports a shared-signal configuration with opposite reference-oriented effects, while a concordant locus, L007, provides an internal directionality contrast.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary global evidence: no-MHC LDSC `rg = −0.1798`, `SE = 0.0665`, `P = 0.0068`, BH-FDR = 0.0113; the rescue analysis remains negative (`rg = −0.164`, `P = 0.0143`).
2. Genome-wide local context: HDL-L found no block with BH-FDR < 0.05 for Advanced AMD × POAG.
3. L006 adjudication: quantitative EUR LD passed; unified SuSiE converged; coloc.susie PP4 = 0.975590; signal-level CS overlap = 10; rs1547014 remains opposite after reference harmonization.
4. Orthogonal local sensitivity: LAVA L006 rho = −0.331961, P = 0.0941787, with a wide interval spanning zero; HDL-L chr22.12 has the same negative direction but P = 0.07058.
5. Internal contrast: L007 has strong shared-signal support but concordant direction (PP4 = 0.967815; LAVA rho = 0.0671874, P = 0.638758), so it is not counted as antagonistic.

## Wording gate

Allowed:

- negative genome-wide genetic correlation;
- opposing genetic architecture;
- candidate antagonistic region;
- candidate opposite-effect shared signal;
- L006 is consistent with a probable antagonistic locus under the prespecified Phase 4 adjudication rules.

Disallowed:

- confirmed antagonistic pleiotropy;
- proven shared causal variant;
- causal mechanism;
- protective or therapeutic target;
- clinical biomarker or clinical utility;
- independent replication.

## Interpretation boundary

The paper should frame L006 as a statistically supported candidate locus whose evidence chain is stronger than the original Tier 2 result because cross-block LD and unified causal-configuration analyses are now available. The remaining uncertainties are independent replication, local power, sample overlap, and the distinction between a shared causal variant and a correlated association pattern.
