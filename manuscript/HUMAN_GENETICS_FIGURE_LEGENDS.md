# Figure legends

**Fig. 1** Global genetic-correlation matrix across AMD and POAG phenotypes. Pairwise no-MHC LDSC estimates for Advanced AMD, Early AMD, overall POAG, the POAG non-IOP component and the POAG IOP component. The Advanced AMD–POAG cell is the primary estimate (rg = −0.1798). POAG component comparisons are descriptive because the component summary statistics are derived from the parent POAG GWAS and are not independent cohorts. AMD, age-related macular degeneration; POAG, primary open-angle glaucoma; IOP, intraocular pressure; LDSC, linkage disequilibrium score regression; MHC, major histocompatibility complex

**Fig. 2** HDL-L local genetic-correlation scan. Finite local rg estimates for Advanced AMD and POAG across 2,463 predefined blocks. Intervals show HDL-L 95% likelihood intervals; missing estimates remain unplotted. The minimum finite BH-FDR was 0.3007 and no block reached BH-FDR < 0.05. HDL-L, high-definition likelihood; rg, genetic correlation; BH-FDR, Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery rate

**Fig. 3** LDSC robustness to chromosome and major-locus exclusion. Advanced AMD–POAG estimates after exclusion of each chromosome in turn, together with the no-MHC estimate and estimates after removal of APOE, ARMS2/HTRA1 or CFH. All chromosome-exclusion estimates remained negative; the displayed range was approximately −0.2209 to −0.1659. These analyses assess direction robustness and are not independent replication analyses. LOCO, leave-one-chromosome-out; SE, standard error

**Fig. 4** Phase 4 local architecture sensitivity. Left, the primary global LDSC estimate and L006 and L007 LAVA estimates with 95% intervals. Right, LAVA estimates compared with overlapping HDL-L blocks. L006 LAVA and HDL-L chr22.12 were both negative, whereas L007 LAVA was positive and HDL-L chr22.16 was weakly negative. No local estimate passed the project-wide BH-FDR threshold. LAVA, Local Analysis of [co]Variant Association; HDL-L, high-definition likelihood

**Fig. 5** Paired direction-resolved evidence adjudication. L006 and L007 were processed with the same quantitative EUR LD and unified SuSiE/coloc.susie workflow. Both had strong shared-signal support, while L006 had opposite harmonized effects and L007 had concordant effects. PP4 and credible-set overlap support a shared-signal configuration under the specified model and do not establish a shared causal variant. CS, credible set; LD, linkage disequilibrium; PP4, posterior probability for a shared causal configuration

**Fig. 6** Direction-resolved architecture summary. Left, counts of the three human-facing final evidence categories among the seven frozen loci. Right, the global LDSC estimate and the L006 and L007 LAVA estimates. The summary shows that a negative global estimate, an opposite-effect candidate shared signal and a concordant shared-signal locus can coexist. The technical Phase 4 tier labels are retained in the supplementary audit and are not the biological interpretation
