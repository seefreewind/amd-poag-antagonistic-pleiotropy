# Human Genetics desk-rejection audit

## A. Does the paper still read as “another secondary analysis of GWAS”?

**Assessment: NO after revision.** The revised story is built around a direction-resolved evidence problem. The manuscript separates three questions that are often conflated: whether the traits have negative genome-wide covariance, whether a locus has a shared association configuration, and whether harmonized effects point in opposite directions. The central contribution is the explicit adjudication of these layers using quantitative LD, multi-signal fine-mapping and colocalization. This is stated in the Introduction, the first Discussion paragraph and the title.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`, Introduction and Discussion; `HUMAN_GENETICS_TITLE_OPTIONS.md`; `HUMAN_GENETICS_CLAIM_AUDIT.tsv`, HG-001 and HG-018

## B. Is the conceptual novelty visible early enough?

**Assessment: YES.** The Abstract opens with the non-equivalence of negative genome-wide correlation and locus-level opposite effects. The Introduction defines the evidence-layer distinction before describing the pipeline. The Discussion opens with three takeaways and repeats the contribution in a single restrained sentence.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_ABSTRACT.md`; `HUMAN_GENETICS_MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`, Introduction and Discussion opening

## C. Is L006 overclaimed?

**Assessment: NO.** L006 is reported as a candidate opposite-effect shared signal. PP4 and credible-set overlap are described as support for a shared-signal configuration, not proof of a shared causal variant. LAVA is reported as directionally consistent but not definitive, and the absence of independent replication is explicit. The phrases “confirmed antagonistic pleiotropy”, “causal mechanism”, “protective effect” and “therapeutic target” are excluded from positive claims.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`, Results and Discussion; `HUMAN_GENETICS_FIGURE_LEGENDS.md`, Fig. 5; `HUMAN_GENETICS_CLAIM_AUDIT.tsv`, HG-004, HG-014, HG-020 and HG-023

## D. Is L007 retained as a meaningful comparator?

**Assessment: YES.** L007 was processed with the same Phase 4 workflow, showed similarly strong shared-signal support, and was concordant in direction. It is presented as an internal contrast and not as a failed analysis or as a positive control.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`, Results; `HUMAN_GENETICS_TABLES.md`, Table 3; `HUMAN_GENETICS_FIGURE_LEGENDS.md`, Fig. 5; `HUMAN_GENETICS_CLAIM_AUDIT.tsv`, HG-005, HG-016 and HG-021

## E. Does the manuscript imply independent validation?

**Assessment: NO.** The rescue AMD resource is explicitly described as related to the primary programme and not an independent replication cohort. LAVA and HDL-L are described as sensitivity or contextual analyses using the same underlying summary-statistics resources. The manuscript states that independent validation remains required.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_MANUSCRIPT_V1.md`, Methods, Results and Limitations; `HUMAN_GENETICS_CLAIM_AUDIT.tsv`, HG-009, HG-010 and HG-025

## F. Is the negative HDL-L result retained and interpreted correctly?

**Assessment: YES.** The manuscript reports all 2,463 blocks, 696 finite estimates, the minimum finite BH-FDR of 0.3007 and the absence of any BH-FDR-significant block. The result is used to constrain the claim rather than omitted.

Evidence files: `HUMAN_GENETICS_TABLES.md`, Table 2; `HUMAN_GENETICS_FIGURE_LEGENDS.md`, Fig. 2; `HUMAN_GENETICS_CLAIM_AUDIT.tsv`, HG-003 and HG-022
