# Human Genetics title options

## Recommended title

**Direction-resolved genetic analysis of age-related macular degeneration and primary open-angle glaucoma identifies a candidate opposite-effect shared signal**

This title foregrounds the paper’s conceptual contribution: separating genome-wide covariance, shared-signal support and harmonized allelic direction. It reports the L006 result as a candidate signal and avoids implying confirmed antagonistic pleiotropy, a shared causal variant or clinical translation.

## Alternative 1

**Dissecting opposing genetic architecture between age-related macular degeneration and primary open-angle glaucoma**

Shorter and more conceptual, but less explicit about the direction-resolved adjudication and the candidate status of L006.

## Alternative 2

**Genome-wide genetic correlation and direction-resolved shared signals between age-related macular degeneration and primary open-angle glaucoma**

Most conservative. It accurately describes the evidence layers but gives less prominence to the paper’s methodological distinction between shared-signal support and effect direction.

## Title decision

Use the recommended title for Human Genetics submission. The phrase “candidate opposite-effect shared signal” is the narrowest wording that captures the frozen L006 evidence chain while preserving the project language gate.
